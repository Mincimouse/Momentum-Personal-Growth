# Momentum

**Goals become systems.**

Momentum is an iOS app for people who want more than a to-do list — goals, habits, health, workouts, and an AI Coach in one place, built around daily momentum instead of guilt.

---

## What you can do

### Today
Start each day with tasks, habits, and a clear picture of what matters. Check things off, keep streaks alive, and see your momentum build over time.

### Goals & habits
Set meaningful goals with milestones and daily habits that actually stick. Track progress, celebrate wins, and adjust when life changes.

### AI Coach
Talk to a coach that knows your goals, habits, and recent activity. Get plans, nudges, and weekly reviews — not generic motivational quotes.

### Health & nutrition
Log meals (including photo-based food logging on supported plans), track calories and macros, and connect health context to your goals.

### Fitness & workouts
Log workouts Hevy-style — exercises, sets, reps, rest timers, and progressive overload suggestions based on your history.

### Insights
See your personal growth score, consistency trends, and patterns across goals, habits, and fitness.

### Friends (optional)
Share progress with friends when you want accountability — moments, workouts, and social feed features.

---

## Who it's for

Momentum is for anyone building long-term change: founders, athletes, students, or anyone tired of starting over every Monday. One app for productivity, health, and training — without juggling five separate tools.
