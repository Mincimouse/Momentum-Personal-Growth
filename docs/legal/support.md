## Common questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Open **Settings** on your iPhone → tap your name → **Subscriptions** → **Momentum** → **Cancel Subscription**. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

### How do I delete my account?

In the app: **Settings → Account → Delete account**. This permanently removes your data from our servers, subject to the retention limits in our Privacy Policy.

### The AI Coach gave wrong advice — what should I do?

Momentum's Coach is for motivation and planning only. It is **not** medical, nutritional, or professional advice. If something looks wrong, don't follow it — email us with details so we can improve guardrails.

### Workouts, habits, or data not syncing?

Make sure you're signed in and online. Force-quit and reopen the app. If it persists, email us with the steps to reproduce.
